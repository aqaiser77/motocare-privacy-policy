2. Permissions Requested & Purpose of Use

The App requests only the permissions strictly necessary to provide vehicle diagnostic features, location-based garage lookup, and ad delivery. You can grant or revoke these runtime permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

Android Permission Category Why It Is Needed & How It Is Used
RECORD_AUDIO Microphone / Audio Acoustic Vehicle Diagnostics: Used solely when you actively tap "Listen to Engine" or record an automotive sound. The App computes acoustic frequency spectra (FFT analysis, peak decibels, dominant frequencies) to detect engine knocking, belt squeals, transmission whine, and bearing friction.

Privacy Guarantee: Audio is processed exclusively for mechanical diagnostic purposes. We do never record ambient conversations, perform voice identification, or conduct background listening.
CAMERA Camera / Visual 360° Visual Vehicle Inspection: Used to scan vehicle components (such as tires, brake rotors, belts, pulleys, exhaust smoke, fluid leaks, and underbody) during visual diagnosis sessions.

Privacy Guarantee: The camera stream is used for real-time edge computer vision (Google ML Kit) and optical diagnosis. Photos or frames are processed only upon your explicit action to diagnose automotive faults. We do not scan faces or extract biometric data.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Location Nearby Garage & Radar Map: Used on-demand to display your vehicle's current position on Google Maps and calculate driving distances to nearby automotive repair workshops, tire centers, and emergency mechanics in your region.

Privacy Guarantee: Location is retrieved only while actively viewing the Garages radar map. Location data is never tracked in the background and is never sold to third-party data brokers.
INTERNET
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 Network Required to fetch map tiles from Google Maps SDK, communicate with Google Gemini AI models for multi-system mechanical diagnostics, and display advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Audio Controls Required to optimize microphone gain and sampling rates for vehicle acoustic recording without distortion.

3. Advertising & Monetization (Google AdMob)

This application is supported by advertising to offer vehicle diagnostic capabilities free of charge. We integrate the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to deliver banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Data Collected by AdMob

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and Privacy Sandbox guidelines, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID): A user-resettable, unique device identifier used for serving relevant ads and frequency capping.
  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen size, language settings, and general network carrier.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, impressions, and engagement metrics for fraud detection and performance analytics.
  • Coarse IP Address: Used to derive non-precise location (such as city or country level) for serving geographically appropriate advertisements.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time directly on your Android device: go to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

5. Local Storage & Offline Capability

Moto Care is built with an offline-first architecture. Your vehicle profiles (vehicle make, model, year, nickname) and recorded diagnostic repair histories are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite Room database. This data remains on your device and is not synchronized to external servers unless you explicitly perform an AI diagnosis request.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We respect your right to control your personal data:

  • Local Vehicle & Diagnostic History: You can delete any vehicle profile or diagnostic report at any time from within the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing app storage via Android Settings permanently purges all local data.
  • Acoustic & Visual Samples: Diagnostic audio waveforms and camera frames are retained temporarily in volatile memory only during the active analysis session and are immediately discarded thereafter.
  • Deletion Requests: If you have any inquiries regarding data stored or processed, you may email us at a.qaiser77@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security Measures

We implement commercial-grade administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your information. All cloud transmissions (including API requests to Google Gemini and Google Maps) are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption. Furthermore, the App does not store sensitive financial details, passwords, or personal identity numbers.
